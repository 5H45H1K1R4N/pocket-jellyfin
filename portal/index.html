Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access photos?

Sign in with your family account using the button at the top right. Each account is granted access to specific photo albums.

Why can't another device connect?

Ensure both devices are on the exact same Wi-Fi SSID. If your router has "AP/Client Isolation" enabled, devices cannot communicate locally.

Is my data private?

Yes. Peppy Home Hub runs strictly on your phone server without transmitting personal photos or files to any external cloud.
