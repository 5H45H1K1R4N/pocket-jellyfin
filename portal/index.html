Help & Getting Started
Frequently Asked Questions
Quick 3-Step Start

1. Connect to Home Wi-Fi: Ensure you are on the primary family Wi-Fi network.

2. Choose Your Service: Tap Movies & TV for cinema, or Minecraft to play.

3. Need Help? Contact the home server admin if you need personal credentials.

Common Questions
Why does a service say "Offline"? ▼
The server runs health checks in real time. If Minecraft or Jellyfin is offline, the service process may simply be stopped. Ask the host to start it via Termux.
The page isn't opening on another device ▼
Ensure you are connected to the exact same home Wi-Fi network. If your router has "Guest Isolation" or "AP Isolation" enabled, devices cannot communicate directly.
Can I access this outside the house? ▼
By design, this hub is locked to the local home Wi-Fi for speed, privacy, and security. Remote access is only possible if the host configures a private tunnel (like Playit).
How do I upload files from my phone? ▼
Go to the File Drop tab, tap the box, choose your photos or videos, and they will upload directly to the server at full Wi-Fi speed.
